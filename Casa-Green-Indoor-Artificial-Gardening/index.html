About Casa Greens Atelier

Crafting Botanical Wonder Where Real Plants Cannot Survive.

Casa Greens is a specialized architectural studio dedicated solely to the curation, structural design, and precision installation of hyper-realistic artificial indoor landscapes.

Real vegetation in climate-controlled modern interiors often struggles with inadequate natural light, air conditioning drafts, soil parasites, and expensive drip-irrigation leaks. We eliminate biological decay while preserving pure, serene biophilic beauty.

Every leaf, bough, and moss specimen is botanically accurate, hand-textured, and fire-retardant tested. We collaborate intimately with interior architects, luxury homeowners, and commercial developers to realize dreamscapes of everlasting life.

How We Work

The Four-Stage Design Journey

From initial concept sketch to white-glove installation, our design journey guarantees absolute creative and structural precision.

01
Phase 01

Consultation & Architectural Audit

We assess your architectural blueprints, sightlines, ceiling heights, existing finishes, and lighting conditions. We discuss botanical species preferences and biophilic objectives.

  • Site spatial audit & laser measurement
  • Botanical style preference discovery
  • Initial scope & budgetary framework
02
Phase 02

3D Concept Design & Botanical Curation

Our design studio produces photorealistic 3D renders, elevation drawings, and physical sample boards showcasing foliage textures, bark finishes, and planter materials.

  • 3D visualization & placement mockups
  • Curated botanical species specimen palette
  • Engineered attachment & weight calculations
03
Phase 03

Artisan Customization & Fabrication

Natural hardwood trunks are treated and hand-sculpted in our studio. Foliage stems are individually shaped, heat-tempered, and grafted to create natural organic asymmetry.

  • Bespoke sculptural branch assembly
  • Fire-retardancy testing and certification
  • Custom planter trough manufacture & finishing
04
Phase 04

Installation & Final Reveal

Our certified installation crew securely anchors structures, arranges groundcover dressings (river stone, bark chips, preserved moss), and conducts final lighting alignment.

  • Off-hours or scheduled clean installation
  • Lighting harmonization and foliage styling
  • Care handbook & long-term craftsmanship warranty
